struct fss::Vdpf< in_bits, Group, Prg, XorHash, Hash, In >::Cw

Correction word.

Layout

According to the paper, there are s, tl, tr to be stored. tl is stored at the clamped bit of s. cws[in_bits] stores the output correction word in s. Its tr is false after Gen() and is ignored by evaluation.

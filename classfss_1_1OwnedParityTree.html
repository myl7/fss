Detailed Description

template<typename Scheme>
class fss::OwnedParityTree< Scheme >

Own a host parity-tree allocation and expose a borrowed, read-only view.

This move-only owner does not copy storage to a GPU. Views remain valid until the allocation is destroyed or replaced, including after ownership is moved.

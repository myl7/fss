class fss::hash::Aes128Ccr< AesMmo >

AES-128 CCR hash H(z) = AES_K(sigma(z)) xor sigma(z).

For 64-bit halves z = (L, R), sigma(z) = (L xor R, L). L is stored in int4::x and int4::y, and R in int4::z and int4::w. The construction uses the linear orthomorphism from Section 7.3 of https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/074. The AES key K belongs to the backend and differs from HalfTree's seed S.

Template Parameters
AesMmoAes128Mmo<1>, Aes128MmoRaw<1>, or Aes128Soft<1>. Execution-space support, context concurrency, and table/context lifetimes are those of the selected backend. Hash() is host/device for Aes128Soft<1> and host-only for Aes128Mmo<1> and Aes128MmoRaw<1>, whose device use fails to build with an unresolved-extern error naming fss_host_only_Aes128Ccr_used_in_device_code.

Detailed Description

template<int in_bits, typename Group, typename Hash, typename In = uint>
struct fss::HalfTreeDpf< in_bits, Group, Hash, In >::Cw

Correction word.

For levels 1..n-1, s is the CW and extra is unused (false). For level n, s stores SetLsb(HCW, LCW_0) and extra stores LCW_1. The final entry cws[in_bits] stores the output correction word in s. Its extra is false after Gen() and is ignored by evaluation.
